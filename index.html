Online Math Tutoring

K–6 • NYC Curriculum • Library-Supported

Why We Exist

We offer online math tutoring that focuses on foundational understanding, critical thinking, and NYC school curriculum alignment. Each session is structured to build confidence and skill in young learners.

What We Cover

From basic arithmetic to early problem-solving strategies, we help K–6 students thrive.

  • Addition & Subtraction
  • Multiplication & Division
  • Fractions & Decimals
  • Word Problems
  • Homework Help

How It Works

Students meet with trained tutors online once or twice a week. Sessions are structured, interactive, and tailored to each child’s pace. Parents receive progress updates.
