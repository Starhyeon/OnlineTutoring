About the Program

Our library student tutoring program provides free, friendly math help for K–6 students following the NYC curriculum. Sessions are led by trained student tutors in a safe, supportive environment.

Math Subjects We Cover

Addition & Subtraction
Multiplication & Division
Fractions & Decimals
Word Problems
NYC Test Prep

Schedule

Tutoring sessions take place weekly at the library. Walk-ins welcome, no cost required.
